## Conversion script (on the pilot-delivery call)

After you walk them through the finished pilot episode:

> "We can keep this running on Autopilot — 3 firesides a week, all 6 channels, same voice, 5 min/wk of your time. $997 a month, cancel in one message. Want me to spin it up?"

**Pricing objections:**
- "Too expensive" → "That's one KOL tweet. You get 130 assets a month."
- "I need to think about it" → "Totally. We pause the pilot output until you decide. If you don't come back in 14 days, we delete the clone."
- "My team can do this in-house" → "They won't. Every founder says this. A ghostwriter does a thread a day — we do 20 assets."

## Target metrics

- 10 DMs sent → 4 replies (40%)
- 4 replies → 3 pilots delivered (75%)
- 3 pilots delivered → 2 Autopilot conversions (66%)
- Expected MRR from week 1: $1,994 ($997 × 2)
- Expected MRR by month 3 with re-touches + upsells: $5–8k
