## 1. What Gravity does

Gravity Labs will:

1. Train an AI voice model ("Voice Clone") from audio samples you publicly published (e.g., a podcast appearance) and/or from a voice sample you record during onboarding.
2. Use the Voice Clone exclusively to produce audio content ("Episodes") that Gravity delivers to you for your review and approval.
3. Host and distribute approved Episodes to the platforms you specify (Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, and related syndication channels), attributed to you and disclosed as AI-produced.
4. Apply an audible AI-disclosure marker at the start or in the show notes of every Episode, consistent with the **FTC's 2025 guidance on AI-generated content disclosure**.

## 2. What Gravity will not do

Gravity will not:

1. Use the Voice Clone to generate content for any party other than you.
2. Sell, lease, or license the Voice Clone to third parties.
3. Use the Voice Clone to impersonate you for fraudulent, deceptive, defamatory, or harassing purposes.
4. Publish any Episode without your prior approval during the pilot period (first 14 days). After that, you may delegate ongoing approval to a default-approve posture with 48-hour review windows, configurable per-client.
5. Retain the Voice Clone after termination of this Agreement beyond the 30-day deletion window specified in Section 5.

## 3. Your warranties

You represent and warrant that:

1. You are the individual whose voice is being cloned, and you have the legal authority to grant the rights in this Agreement.
2. The voice samples you provide or permit Gravity to use are your own voice, and your use of them does not infringe any third party's rights.
3. You have authority to enter this Agreement on behalf of any business entity referenced in the intake form (e.g., to permit attribution of Episodes to your project).

## 4. Your rights

You retain:

1. Full copyright ownership of all Episodes produced by Gravity using your Voice Clone. Gravity holds no rights to distribute Episodes outside the channels you authorize.
2. The right to cancel this Agreement at any time by a single written message (email or Telegram) to Gravity.
3. The right to request immediate deletion of the Voice Clone at any time.
4. The right to retain all produced Episodes, source audio, and assets after cancellation. Gravity will not remove published Episodes unless you request it.

## 5. Deletion and retention

1. **On your cancellation:** Gravity will delete the Voice Clone within **thirty (30) days** of your written cancellation request. Produced Episodes remain on whatever platforms they were published to unless you request takedown.
2. **On cold-outreach non-response:** If Gravity cloned your voice as part of a cold outreach pilot (e.g., a personalized demo clip accompanying an initial contact message) and you do not engage within **fourteen (14) days**, Gravity will automatically delete the Voice Clone. This is part of our standard outreach protocol.
3. **On Cofounder Test failure:** If the Cofounder Test (Section 6) fails, Gravity will delete the Voice Clone within **seven (7) days** and refund the pilot fee in full.

## 6. Cofounder Test

The pilot fee ($497) is fully refundable contingent on the following fidelity check:

1. Before Gravity starts monthly billing, Gravity will produce a 30-second blind A/B audio clip containing half your real voice and half Voice Clone output, cut together in an undisclosed order.
2. By default, Gravity will send the clip to three podcast-listeners from Gravity's review network who have no prior knowledge of you. Alternatively, you may name three of your own contacts.
3. Each listener will be asked to identify which half of the clip is the Voice Clone.
4. If **two or more of the three listeners correctly identify the Voice Clone**, the Test has failed.
5. On failure: (a) Gravity refunds the $497 pilot fee in full via Stripe, (b) Gravity deletes the Voice Clone within 7 days, (c) you retain any Episodes already produced and the underlying audio assets.
6. On success (fewer than two listeners identify the Voice Clone): the pilot fee credits toward Month 1 of Autopilot billing, and ongoing monthly billing begins on the date of the first Autopilot episode ("Episode 5" of your feed).

## 7. Disclosure and platform compliance

Every Episode produced under this Agreement will include:

1. An audible or written AI-disclosure marker in the episode's opening segment or show notes, consistent with FTC 2025 AI-content rules and with Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube's published AI-content policies as of April 2026.
2. Attribution of Gravity Labs as the producer (e.g., "Produced by Gravity Broadcast").
3. The full text of this disclosure policy is published at https://gravitylabs.xyz/legal/ai-disclosure (or equivalent URL to be provided on request).

## 8. Fees and billing

1. **Pilot fee:** $497 USD, charged via Stripe Checkout at the time of this Agreement's execution. Refundable per Section 6.
2. **Autopilot monthly fee:** $997 USD/month, billed month-to-month via Stripe, starting with the 5th produced Episode. Month 1 of Autopilot is billed at $500 (with the $497 pilot fee credited). Month 2 and onward are billed at the full $997.
3. **No long-term commitment:** Autopilot is month-to-month. You may cancel at any time via a single written message. Gravity will not bill for any subsequent month after a cancellation message is received.
4. **Refunds:** Pilot fee is refundable per Section 6. Monthly Autopilot fees are not prorated or refundable for a cancellation received after the billing date.

## 9. Liability and indemnification

1. Gravity's liability to you under this Agreement is capped at the total fees you have paid Gravity in the 12 months preceding any claim.
2. You agree to indemnify Gravity for claims arising from your own misuse of Episodes produced under this Agreement, including but not limited to: publishing Episodes in jurisdictions or on platforms that prohibit AI-generated content, using Episodes to violate a third party's rights, or distributing Episodes that contain content you directed Gravity to produce that violates applicable law.
3. Gravity makes no warranty that the Voice Clone will pass the Cofounder Test. The Cofounder Test exists precisely because Voice Clone fidelity is not guaranteed, and the refund mechanism is your protection against fidelity failure.

## 10. Revocation

This Agreement is revocable by you at any time on written notice. Revocation triggers the deletion timeline in Section 5(1). Revocation does not retroactively invalidate Episodes produced before the revocation date.
